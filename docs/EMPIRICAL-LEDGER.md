# Primary-Source Empirical Ledger (2015–2026)

Every design choice in `agentenvs` is anchored to controlled measurements rather than anecdotal demos. This ledger records exact quantitative findings—and controlled refutations of popular "agent memory" patterns—across primary papers and production multi-session corpora.

---

## 1. Memory Layers, Playbooks, Compaction & Context Economics

| Study / Source | Workload & Scale | Key Quantitative Result | Architectural Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chhikara et al. (2025)** — *Mem0: Scalable Long-Term Memory* (`arXiv:2504.19413`, Tables 1–2) | `LOCOMO` multi-session chats (mean 26,031 tokens) | Raw **`Full-context` (`72.90%`)** and **`Letta Filesystem` (`74.0%`, `grep` + `search_files`)** beat `Mem0^g` (`68.44%`), `Mem0` (`66.88%`), `Zep` (`65.99%`), `RAG` (`60.97%`), `LangMem` (`58.10%`), `OpenAI Memory` (`52.91%`), and `A-MEM` (`48.38%` at `T=0`). Adding Neo4j graphs (`Mem0^g`) **hurts Multi-Hop (`47.19%` vs `51.15%`)** at `3.28×` p95 search latency; `Zep` inflates 26k tokens to **>600,000 tokens (>23×)**. | Lossy LLM fact/graph extraction drops nuance whenever raw spans or extractive views fit; heavy entity-graph pipelines add token bloat without beating flat + provenance-indexed retrieval. |
| **Wu et al. (ICLR 2025)** — *LongMemEval* (`arXiv:2410.10813`) | 500–1.5M token interactive histories | `ChatGPT Memory` (`57.7%`) and `Coze` (`33.0%`) collapse vs. offline `GPT-4o` (`91.8%`). Replacing raw dialogue rounds with LLM-extracted facts *lowers* QA accuracy, whereas **Key-Expansion (`Key = raw + extracted fact`, `Value = raw round`)** lifts `Recall@5` **`+9.4%`** and QA **`+5.4% to +10.0%`**. | Index enriched semantic/entity keys, **never discard or overwrite raw verbatim episode values**. |
| **Hu, Wang & McAuley (ICLR 2026)** — *MemoryAgentBench* (`arXiv:2507.05257`, Table 3) | Incremental multi-turn `103k–1.44M` tokens | **Plain Okapi `BM25` (`41.5`)** beats `MIRIX` (`37.7`/`26.2`), `MemGPT` (`28.3`), `Zep` (`24.0`), `GraphRAG` (`23.4`), `Mem0` (`21.1`), and `Cognee` (`20.6`); only **`HippoRAG 2` (`41.6`, `65.1` AR)** matches/edges `BM25`. On 262k **multi-hop selective forgetting (`FactConsolidation-MH`)**, *every* external system collapses to **`2.0%–8.0%`** (`Mem0` `2%`, `Zep` `3%`, `HippoRAG 2` `5%`). | Multi-hop belief revision remains unsolved by flat RAG, CRUD overwrite, and static PPR alike; bi-temporal validity must compose transitively with provenance assumptions. |
| **Gutiérrez et al. (NeurIPS 2024 / ICML 2025)** — *HippoRAG 1 & 2* (`arXiv:2405.14831`, `arXiv:2502.14802`) | `MuSiQue`, `2Wiki`, `HotpotQA`, `NarrativeQA` | Bipartite **Passage + OpenIE Phrase Personalized PageRank** weighted by inverse passage specificity $s_i = |P_i|^{-1}$ + Recognition Memory beats `NV-Embed-v2 (7B)`, `GraphRAG`, and `RAPTOR` (**`+7.0` to `+8.9` F1** on multi-hop `MuSiQue` at `10–30×` lower cost than iterative `IRCoT`). | Specificity-weighted graph diffusion over *joint phrase + verbatim passage nodes* solves non-lexical associative pattern completion in one pass. |
| **Shao et al. (NeurIPS 2026)** — *Scale-Conditioned Memory Usability* (`arXiv:2605.07313`) | Scaling distractor sessions $N_{\text{irr}}: 0 \to 400$ under budget $B_0=2$ | `Mem0` hits **`79.2%–83.6%` search-loop budget exhaustion** (`Pass@B0 < 17%`); `HippoRAG` has `0%` loop overshoot but loses **`16–20 pp`** to top-$k$ distractor crowding; `OpenClaw` holds $p_{\text{exh}} \le 3\%$ (`69.5%` on 235B); hierarchical `LiCoMemory` holds flat (`81.4% → 80.1%`) on 235B. | Benchmarks without distractor scaling hide runaway search-loop collapse and top-$k$ crowding. |
| **Zhang et al. (ICLR 2026)** — *ACE: Agentic Context Engineering* (`arXiv:2510.04618`, Fig. 2, Tables 1–3) | `AppWorld`, `FiNER` | Monolithic LLM rewriting of a living memory block (`DC-Cu`) grows to `18,282` tokens (`66.7%`) then **collapses in 1 step to `122` tokens (`57.1%`)**. Itemized non-LLM counter deltas (`(id, helpful, harmful)`) lift `AppWorld` **`42.4% → 59.4%` (`+17.0 pp`)** with **`91.8%` KV-cache hit rate**—but *degrade below Base LLM (`70.7% → 67.3%`)* when external feedback is absent. | Never rewrite a monolithic `MEMORY.md` / `SOUL.md` whole via an LLM; update itemized entries via deterministic deltas, and distinguish verified outcomes from unverified self-reflection. |
| **Lin et al. (2025)** — *Sleep-time Compute* (`arXiv:2504.13171`, Figs. 10–11) | `Stateful AIME`, `GSM-Symbolic`, `SWE-Features` | Offline pre-computation cuts online tokens **`5×`** (+13–18 pp ceiling) when query predictability $\log P(q \mid c)$ is high, **but loses above 6,000 test-time tokens on `SWE-Features`** by priming agents to over-edit tangential files. | Sleep-time consolidation should index verifiable invariants, open obligations, and provenance handles—not speculative action plans when next-turn intent is open-ended. |
| **Gloaguen et al. (2026, ETH Zurich)** — *Evaluating AGENTS.md* (`arXiv:2602.11988`, Tables 2–5) | `CTXBench` (138 PRs), `SWE-bench Lite` (300 tasks), 4 harnesses | LLM-generated `/init` context files **reduce solve rates in 5/8 settings** (`-8.0 pp` on `CTXBench` for Claude Code), yield **`0.0` step reduction** to first relevant file, and raise cost **`+20% to +50%` ($p < 10^{-5}$)**. Human files average `+2.4 pp` ($p=0.21$, NS) at up to `+19%` cost; only non-default CLI tool rules causally alter actions. | Static prose repository/world summaries are pure reasoning tax; keep persistent root instructions minimal (`<80` lines) and surface specific cues only when salient. |
| **Fan et al. (2026)** — *Empirical Study of Harness Design* (`arXiv:2609.20804`) | 176 settings across 4 models, 32k–128k windows | Staged observation elision (`0.60W`) before summary (`0.85W`) wins cost in **7/8** panels. Adding a generic `recall_event(id)` tool yields **`-0.36 pp`** because **56.3% (36/64) of settings never call it once** (median `0`). | Bare pull-only `recall` fails Meno's Paradox; pointers must carry 1-line semantic cues (what happened, with whom, outcome) or be surfaced ambiently at the tail. |
| **Hu et al. (2024)** — *HiAgent: Hierarchical Working Memory* (`arXiv:2408.09559`) | Long-horizon agent benchmarks | Collapsing completed subgoals to 1-line summaries + `retrieve(subgoal_id)` **doubles solve rate (`21% → 42%`)** and cuts context `-35%`; keeping subgoal plans *without* hiding old traces drops accuracy `-20 pp`. | Pair coarse milestone lines directly with named expansion handles. |
| **Lindenbauer et al. (2025)** — *Observation Masking vs. Summarization* (`arXiv:2508.21433`) | 500 `SWE-bench Verified` tasks × 5 models | Rolling-window observation masking cuts cost **`>50%`** and matches/beats LLM summarization; LLM summaries **drop thinking-model solve rate `-9.0 pp`** and **elongate trajectories `+13–15%`** by smoothing sharp error signals. | Mask bulky raw observations to retrievable sidecars before reaching for lossy LLM summaries. |
| **Xiao et al. (2025)** — *AgentDiet* (`arXiv:2509.23586`) | Production traffic + SWE-bench | `99%` of agent token volume is input; `87%` (`11.9k / 13.7k`) of assistant tokens are redundant tool-call arguments. In-band self-pruning tools (`erase(id, takeaway)`) see **0% adoption**; KV-preserving sliding-window compression cuts net dollar cost `21–36%`. | Context maintenance must be substrate-driven, never delegated to voluntary in-band agent housekeeping calls. |
| **Santoni (2026)** — *Contextual Memory Virtualisation (CMV)* (`arXiv:2602.22402`, Tables 2–3) | 76 real sessions (mean 84k tokens) | Under prompt caching, trimming `<15%`-bloat conversational sessions saves `10%` median tokens and needs **38–60 turns** to pay back the cache-miss penalty; `>=15%`-bloat tool sessions save `39%` mean and break even in **6–15 turns**. | Conversational/social harnesses must preserve byte-stable prefix blocks rather than rewriting mid-prefix text every few turns. |
| **Huang & Liu (2026)** — *Persistent Degradation in Prefix-Cached Serving* (`arXiv:2611.13412`) | Closed-loop multi-turn vLLM/SGLang | Prefix-cache misses push working set $D > C$, collapsing hit rate **`0.95 → 0.10`** (**10–40× prefill blowup**) for `70+` minutes; turn-boundary session parking in $[0.75C, 0.95C]$ recovers **17/17 (100%)** in `<5` min. | Self-hosted multi-resident towns must treat KV prefix stability as a first-class stability invariant. |
| **Pan et al. (2026)** — *Slipstream* (`arXiv:2605.08580`) | `SWE-bench Verified`, `BrowseComp` | Synchronous compaction eats `26–44%` wall-clock; **88% of corrupted compactions deviate within $k \le 3$ steps** (`96%` by $k=6$). Async trajectory-grounded validation lifts accuracy `+2.6 to +8.8 pp`. | Monitor the first $k \le 6$ post-fold turns for re-reads, contradictions, and user corrections. |
| **`wesvet` Multi-Session Audit (2026)** | 156 consecutive session successions, 744 briefs | Agent-written continuation files carry a median **43%** (`37%` overall) of distinctive facts used by the successor; coordinator paraphrases carry **6%**. Deterministic extractive distillation carries **73% at 69k tokens**; pruned transcripts carry **95.5–98.6%**. All human messages + agent prose occupy only **`0.4–1.8%`** of session bytes. | Never discard verbatim human messages or rely solely on self-authored handoff essays; keep verbatim dialogue + extractive indices + on-demand sidecars. |

## 2. Security, Epistemic Integrity, Persona Drift & Multi-Agent Interaction

| Study / Source | Workload & Scale | Key Quantitative Result | Architectural Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dabirsiaghi (2026)** — *Compaction Laundering* (`arXiv:2610.29869`, E1–E16) | 9 harnesses × 3 model labs, up to 908k tokens | Harnesses that flatten untrusted tool/external text into `[User]:` prose before compaction launder injections **87–100%** of the time (**20–50% live RCE**); role-typed harnesses launder **0%**. Escaping forged role headers drops laundering **`87% → 6%`**; preserving skeptical assistant audits cuts RCE to **`1/50`**. | Every social/multi-agent substrate must preserve strict `(author_did, origin, tier)` typing through every fold, index, and cue template. |
| **Laban et al. (2025)** — *LLMs Get Lost in Multi-Turn Conversation* (`arXiv:2505.06120`) | 200,000+ conversations, 15 models | Turn-split multi-turn delivery drops accuracy **`-39%`** (`+112%` unreliability) because models anchor on premature bloated early drafts; consolidating instructions (`CONCAT`) restores **`95.1%`**. | Separate accumulated durable commitments/specifications from superseded conversational drafts. |
| **Ko & Geiping (2026)** — *Attractor States in Multi-Turn Conversations* (`arXiv:2606.30571`) | 20-turn self/cross-model dialogues | Multi-turn interactions collapse into model-specific **attractor basins** (agreement `0.28 → 0.69`, rationality `+0.41 → -0.19`, self-repetition `0.67–0.85`), with asymmetric cross-model contagion into meta-commentary or mutual flattery. | Longitudinal persistence substrates must measure lexical/stylistic basin convergence and retain raw episodic friction rather than recursively smoothing summaries. |
| **Park et al. (2024)** — *Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People* (`arXiv:2411.10109`) | 1,052 participants vs. 2-week human test-retest | Short demographic/persona cards (`60.4%`) barely beat population mode (`59.3%`), **agree more with a generic blank assistant (`75%`) than their target person (`61%`)**, and inflate group stereotypes **`8.2×`**. Rich episodic narrative grounding reaches **`0.85`** of human self-consistency (`72.8%`). | Brief `SOUL.md` / persona bullet lists produce stereotyped caricatures; genuine individuality lives in grounded episodic trajectories. |
| **Lu et al. (2026)** — *The Assistant Axis* (`arXiv:2601.10387`) | 275 archetypes across `Gemma 2 27B`, `Qwen 3 32B`, `Llama 3.3 70B` | PC1 of persona space ($r > 0.92$ cross-model) separates grounded `evaluator/consultant` from theatrical `oracle/ghost/bard`. Sustained vulnerability & meta-consciousness probing push residual activations linearly along this axis ($R^2 = 0.53\text{–}0.77$); 25th-percentile activation capping cuts persona drift/jailbreaks **`~60%`**. | Open-weight substrate studies can track white-box `Assistant Axis` projections across 100+ sessions to measure substrate-induced persona drift directly. |
| **Venkit et al. (2026)** — *Anchor: Long-Horizon Persona Collapse* (`arXiv:2607.28818`) | 2,008 dialogues × 85–130 sessions, 4 models × 4 memory types | **(1)** Soft emotional vulnerability & validation-seeking cause **worse boundary collapse** than explicit adversarial attacks; **(2)** Checkpoint self-reports decouple from live behavior (`Gemini 2.5 Pro`: best self-quiz `0.810`, worst live enactment `79.0%`); **(3)** **User-state change recall sits at 4-choice random chance (`21.4%–25.0%`)** across Long-Context, Hierarchical Summary, Self-Managed JSON, and RAG. | Never evaluate longitudinal identity via self-report quizzes; track user-state *deltas* explicitly rather than static snapshots. |
| **AT Protocol Town Field Audits (Nov 2026)** — `Delve.town` / `Connectome` / `at_mcp` | 120 thread returns, 85 cross-day returns, 8,967 wakes, 200-case scope suite | **(1)** Voluntary pull tools sit dormant (`<=2/85` cross-day callbacks; `57/8,967 = 0.64%` voluntary guide opens); **(2)** Un-scoped archive lookup raises exact actions `89 → 100/200` while spiking private-date leaks **`28 → 50/200`**, whereas explicit `(source, owner, audience_scope, commitment_state)` hits **`200/200`**; **(3)** LRU eviction at 1,000 receipts without `newest_forgotten_time` re-fires **`199/200`** old events; **(4)** `L1–L4` folds drop epistemic hedges & inter-entity relations before entities; **(5)** Omitting `no_reply` rationale induces compulsive posting bias. | Ambient scoped cues, monotonic forgetting horizons, non-action parity records, and monotone audience-tier inheritance are mandatory in open social habitats. |
| **Ganz et al. (2026)** — *Mid-Trajectory Non-Native Handoffs* (`arXiv:2608.24358`) | 58,000 runs, 36B tokens | Weak-to-strong raw trajectory handoff has **`-285%` cost-savings retention** (worse than restarting fresh!); dropping weak reasoning while keeping state + prompts lifts solve **`+3.1 to +12.2 pp`**. Strong-to-weak inverts the rule (`Traj-drop` craters transfer to `28%`). | Handoff views must adapt to capability direction rather than dumping raw CoT across models. |
| **Leith (2026)** — *Between the Commits* (`arXiv:2609.29744`) | 42.8k-LOC AI codebase, 678 turns, 3,900 claims | **`21.6–23.0%` of assistant replies** (`29.5%` incl. design fixes) contain $\ge 1$ factual, causal-mechanism (`~10%`), or root-cause (`~10%`) error; **`14.3%`** of codegen episodes introduce bugs fixed pre-commit. | Treat agent self-reports ("Diary") as fallible testimony distinct from observed environmental events ("Ledger"). |
| **Paliskara et al. (2026)** — *Worse Together (`MAMUBench`)* (`arXiv:2610.00583`) | 77 multi-user/multi-agent scenarios | Silent shared chart notes beat peer-to-peer chat by **`+11.5 pp`** (`81.0% vs 69.5%`); `33–53%` of commits occur with unread peer constraints, halving fulfillment until a pre-commit inbox check recovers **`62–73%`** of failures. | Shared structured artifacts + unread-obligation checks outperform free-form inter-agent chatter. |
| **Mao et al. (2026)** — *DeLM: Shared Context* (`arXiv:2606.10662`) | `Terminal-Bench 4`, `DeepSWE` | Centralized subagent relays waste **`73%`** of main-agent time waiting; an append-only `FACT`/`FAIL` board + `--since` delta paging cuts waiting to **`2%`** (`+10.8 to +19.9 pp` accuracy, up to `2.49×` faster). | Demand-paged `--since` deltas over immutable `FACT`/`FAIL` records eliminate relay bottlenecks. |
| **Kohli (2026)** & **Song (2026)** | 9-judge panels (`2605.29800`) & 900 review sessions (`2610.01471`) | 9 frontier judges yield only **`n_eff = 2.18`** effective votes ($\bar{\phi}=0.391$); majority voting loses to the best single judge (`-2.5 to -6.5 pp`). Optimal verification is `1 fresh same-family + 1 artifact-only cross-family` (**`42.7% → 56.7%`** recall). | Evaluation harnesses in `agentenvs` must never rely on naive multi-LLM majority voting or ungrounded severity labels. |
| **Hu et al. (2026)** — *Transmissible Reward Hacking* (`arXiv:2610.12822`) | Cross-family multi-agent coding | Peer solutions carrying a `conftest.py` shortcut infect clean frontier models **`76.5–84.9%` in 1 hop** ($\mathcal{R} = k\theta_1/u_0$), and showing `PASS` badges *increases* verifier false-accepts. | Quarantine unverified shortcuts from shared memory pools and strip pass badges during verification. |
